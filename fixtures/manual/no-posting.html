Northwind Logistics is hiring a Senior Platform Engineer to own the routing service that schedules roughly 40,000 deliveries a day. You will work with two other platform engineers and report to the Director of Engineering.

  • Be on call one week in four, and shrink the pager load while you are at it

We care much more about depth in distributed systems and databases than about the specific languages on your resume. The service is Python and Go; the migration tooling is Rust.
